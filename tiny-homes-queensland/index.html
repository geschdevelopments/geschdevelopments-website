04

Common questions

Do I need Council approval for a tiny home in Queensland?

It depends on the property, intended use and applicable planning, building, plumbing and local requirements. A property-specific review helps identify the right pathway before you commit.

Does it matter if my tiny home is on wheels?

Yes. Whether it is on wheels or fixed to land, how it will be used and its connection to services can affect the requirements that apply.

Can I use a tiny home as a secondary dwelling?

A tiny home may be considered for use as a secondary dwelling, but the applicable requirements depend on the property and proposal. Obtain property-specific advice before proceeding.

Where can I read the official Queensland guidance?

Queensland Government guidance provides a useful starting point. Read the Queensland Planning tiny homes guidance.
