Small businesses

Planning support for a business premise that works.

Whether you are opening, relocating, expanding or changing how a site is used, we help identify the planning questions early so you can move forward with greater clarity.

Business premises

A considered starting point

Understand the pathway before you commit.

Commercial and small-business proposals can involve planning requirements that are easy to overlook when a lease, fit-out or opening date is approaching. We provide practical guidance on the relevant pathway for the property and your proposed use.

Early advice can help you understand what is likely to be needed and coordinate the next steps with confidence.
