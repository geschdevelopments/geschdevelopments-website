Dual occupancies

If resale is a priority, a dual occupancy may be a better option than a granny flat.

A dual occupancy can also be a strong option where subdivision is not possible but you want to maximise the yield of your property. The right pathway depends on your goals, what the site can accommodate and the approvals that apply.

01

Resale, yield & flexibility

A practical comparison

When resale and yield matter, explore the full potential of the site.

A granny flat, often called a secondary dwelling, can be an excellent solution for family accommodation, multigenerational living and changing needs. It is generally the smaller, subordinate dwelling associated with a main home on the same lot.

A dual occupancy is designed as two dwellings. It may be worth exploring when a subdivision is not achievable, but you still want to create two independent homes and maximise the potential yield of the property.

02

What to consider

01

Infrastructure & services

Think through water, electricity, internet, access, parking and waste services early. Infrastructure charges may also apply, depending on the proposal and the relevant authority requirements, and can be an important part of the feasibility picture.

02

Rental & intended use

Queensland changes allow a lawful secondary dwelling to be rented to anyone, but development conditions, building standards and the intended use still matter. A property check helps identify the right pathway before you commit.

03

Future flexibility

A secondary dwelling remains connected to the main dwelling on the same lot and is not a standalone property to separately title and sell. A dual occupancy may offer a different long-term option, subject to the site, approval pathway and any future titling strategy.

The better investment is personal

Choose the option that works for your family and your future plans.

A lower-cost granny flat may be exactly the right answer for a parent, adult child or an adaptable family home. A dual occupancy may be the more suitable direction where resale, independent dwellings and property yield are central to the long-term plan.

Neither option is automatically better. Zoning, site area, access, infrastructure capacity and charges, overlays, existing development and council requirements all influence what can be achieved.
